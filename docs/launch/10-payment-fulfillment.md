# Launch PR-09 — durable fulfillment and reconciliation

PAY-02. Signed raw-body webhook intake remains enabled independently of checkout flags. A unique event ledger records only provider identifiers and operational status. Processing retrieves current provider objects under a PostgreSQL transaction lock, verifies mode, order identity, version hash, price, quantity, amount, currency and customer, and commits the exact entitlement plus a unique outbox item atomically. Only complete, paid sessions fulfill. Unpaid asynchronous completion grants nothing; subsequent success, failure, expiration and retries are safe. Unknown products are ignored; malformed known orders fail closed for investigation.

Subscription/invoice notifications retrieve current subscription state rather than applying stale event snapshots. Paid-through requires a paid matching invoice; cancellation preserves remaining access, past-due denies, and verified recovery restores it. Refund/dispute handlers retrieve current payment state: full refunds revoke an exact purchase, partial refunds retain it, open/lost disputes block, won disputes can restore. Subscription refund holds apply to the refunded latest invoice; dispute holds persist until resolved. These are proposed access rules; Terms/commercial/legal approval remains required.

`GET /api/cron/reconcile-payments` uses a dedicated >=32-character CRON_SECRET, retries two oldest events and reconciles two pending/subscription orders per call. Register an appropriate schedule after staging validation. It recovers known session state and searches a bounded provider creation window for a session-publication crash. Ambiguous/paginated recovery stays blocked for operator review. It never initiates a new payment. Inspect backlog before increasing throughput.

Outbox dispatch, receipt attachment, verified guest claiming and delivery retries are PR-10. A pending outbox does not mean an email was sent. No customer mail is sent from the webhook. Keep checkout disabled until delivery and guest recovery are deployed, prices and policy approved, and real Stripe sandbox lifecycle scenarios pass. Local fixtures do not substitute for provider sandbox evidence or production migration/reconciliation.
